01 Who we are

A family-run contractor trusted by national names.

Colmor Construction was established in April 2021, but our grounding in the industry goes back much further. Our management team has spent over 40 years in construction, and we have built a business that adapts to an ever-changing market while keeping the same standards on every scheme.

We work alongside some of the largest national contractors in the country on commercial, residential and infrastructure projects. Over 90% of that work comes through repeat business and reputation — which is exactly how we want it.

  • A directly managed, competent team on every project
  • Continued investment in our workforce and plant
  • One consistent service, no matter the size of scheme

05 How we work

A clear route from
enquiry to handover.

No surprises, no loose ends. Four straightforward stages, managed by one accountable team.

  1. 01

    Enquiry & review

    Send us the drawing package or a description of the works. We review scope, access and programme constraints, then advise on buildability.

  2. 02

    Method & price

    A clear, itemised price with a method statement, programme and resource plan. Relevant technical support and guidance included.

  3. 03

    Delivery

    Our own teams and plant on site, supervised daily. Progress, quality and safety reported as the works proceed.

  4. 04

    Handover

    Snagging, testing and certification completed before we leave. Aftercare and repeat support when the next phase lands.
